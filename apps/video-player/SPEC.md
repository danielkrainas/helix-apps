# Video Player

Drop-in replacement for Showtime.

## Purpose
Play video files.

## Main windows
- Video view with controls.
- Fullscreen.

## Contracts used
- XDG portals: FileChooser, OpenURI, Inhibit.
- MPRIS.
- GStreamer or equivalent.
- GSettings for playback preferences.

## Non-goals
- Full media library.
